Case file 01 — Change management

Change Analysis, Actions and Results

Projects A and B · Hypercare to Sustainment, May–October 2024

01Situation

Context

  • Context: Projects A and B went live in May 2024.
  • I joined the project in June 2024 as Organization Change Management (OCM) Lead.
  • First action after understanding the scenario, as OCM Lead: I started meetings with leadership from the impacted areas, to assess adoption and usage.
02Diagnosing Adoption Gaps with Impacted Areas
20 meetings with leadership from impacted areas

Scenario identified for both projects during hypercare:

  • High risk of business errors, rework and customer impact
  • Difficulty using the new systems
  • Trainings didn't reflect real operational scenarios
  • Frequent bugs that made the system unusable for day-to-day activities
  • Several issues required workarounds, which often failed
  • System instability impacted both suppliers and end customers

03Root Cause

Captured feedback during interviews with impacted areas

There was no clear communication when the process was implemented. There was no structure for escalations or testing.

We need to present the process, along with a communication plan, timeline, and dedicated training for each team.

The process must be properly mapped, tested, and corrected before go-live.

The system doesn't work — we feel like we're just completing tasks to check a box.

These comments helped me understand what was missing before go-live: clear communication, structured testing, and dedicated training for each team.

04My Proposed Actions as Change Management Lead
  1. 1

    Clarify the process flow and what has already been implemented, including next key deliverables. Responsible: Project Team

  2. 2

    Identify and address the problems raised by each area. Responsible: Project Team

  3. 3

    Based on the findings, reinforce training sessions to improve process understanding. Responsible: OCM (structure) + Project Team (apply)

  4. 4

    Reinforce key communications and guidance for bug reporting. Responsible: OCM

05Strategic Alignment — Training Reinforcement Plan Execution
8 strategic alignment meetings, OCM and Project Team
17 reinforcement training sessions delivered
+200 retrained end users

The project team worked closely with the impacted users to clarify processes and resolve errors. The partnership between OCM and the project teams made it possible to create targeted trainings, focused on business scenarios for both Project A and Project B, including real use cases and flows.

After the proposed actions were implemented, users reported feeling more engaged and found the training more effective. OCM completed the change sustainment activities, and the projected negative ROI was reverted. Three months later, help desk tickets showed that the usage of the new systems had increased from 40% to 90%.
